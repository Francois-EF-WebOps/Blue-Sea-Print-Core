# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through focused issues and pull requests. The repository owner retains release authority; a contribution is not accepted until reviewed and merged.

## Start with a bounded task
1. Open or identify an issue describing one problem, outcome and acceptance criteria.
2. Check existing product specifications, learning standards and asset records before creating a duplicate.
3. Ask before changing canonical characters, identity assets, product scope or release status.
4. Work on a branch and submit a pull request. Do not push directly to `main`.

## Learning and product quality
- Distinguish observed facts, interpretation, hypotheses and creative narrative.
- Identify the intended age range, learning objective and evidence of learning.
- Use the established eight-stage Learning Delivery Standard where applicable: Connect, Explain, Model, Guide, Practise, Apply, Reflect and retrieve, Check.
- Make characters instructional only when they demonstrate, question, guide or explain; do not add them as decoration.
- Include accessibility, print and small-screen considerations in acceptance criteria.
- Do not claim field validation, educational effectiveness or commercial readiness without evidence.

## Safety and rights
- The coastal survival protocol is explicitly unvalidated. Do not present it as emergency, survival, medical or safety guidance, and do not market it for those purposes.
- Record source, creator, licence, consent, generation/edit history and permitted uses for every contributed visual or media asset.
- Do not upload third-party assets unless their rights and permissions allow the intended use.
- Do not assume that repository visibility grants commercial reuse rights.
- Do not publish personal data, children's private information, credentials or confidential material.

## Review and acceptance
A pull request must state the issue/task, files changed, rationale, tests or checks performed, visual evidence where relevant, rights/provenance status, known limitations and rollback considerations. Request review for changes to canonical identity, characters, learning claims, safety-related content or release status.

## Contribution and ownership
Describe each contributor's actual contribution. Do not make blanket claims that all contributors assign or waive rights. Licensing or ownership changes require explicit agreement and a recorded decision.

## Definition of done
The acceptance criteria pass; content and assets are reviewed; provenance and rights are recorded; relevant checks pass; limitations are documented; and a maintainer approves and merges the change.
