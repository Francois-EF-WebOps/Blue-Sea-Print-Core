Coastal
Observation Journal

A small notebook for noticing patterns in the place where land meets sea.

Learning goal

I can notice and describe what I see without disturbing it.

Today’s intention

Look carefully. Record what you actually observe. Leave living things and the place undisturbed.

Observation or interpretation?
“A small bird is standing near the water” describes what you can see. “It is looking for food” is an interpretation unless you have more evidence.

Before you begin

I am in a lawful, stable observation area.
I can observe without entering the water or climbing.
I have checked posted warnings and will follow local instructions.
A responsible adult is present when children need supervision.
Stop rule: If waves, weather, tides, visibility, footing, crowds or any other condition feels unsafe, stop and move to a safer place. This journal does not provide tide, weather, navigation or emergency advice.

Notice without disturbing

Choose a few things visible from where you are safely standing. Use your own words. If you do not know what something is, describe its shape, colour, movement or location rather than guessing a name.
