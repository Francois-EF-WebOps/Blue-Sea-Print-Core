# Brand and Educational Positioning

**Status:** Working brand-governance record for Blue-Sea-Print-Core.

## Identity hierarchy

1. **Confluence Helix** — established master identity and overarching publisher identity.
2. **Blue-Sea-Print-Core** — publishing label and source of truth for this product family.
3. **Individual products** — each has its own audience, purpose, format, learning outcomes, safety boundaries and release record.

Do not create a competing master identity or duplicate publisher brand for an individual product. Preserve the existing visual identity and use its approved assets; do not invent replacement logos or visual systems as part of product development.

## Product and audience architecture

The catalogue contains different products for different audiences. Define the audience for each product and edition separately, including age/grade where relevant, reading/language needs, prior knowledge, context, supervision and delivery format.

Do not:
- assign one age range to the entire catalogue;
- market every product as suitable for everyone;
- copy audience assumptions from one product into another;
- create a new brand solely to distinguish audience editions unless the existing brand system requires it.

## Characters

Fin and Nik are the established recurring character pair. They may support continuity and storytelling when they serve a clear learning purpose. They are not mandatory in every product and must not be inserted merely for decoration. Preserve their established identity and approved visual references when they are used.

## Educational inspiration and independence

The Scouts movement is one inspiration for the broader educational system, particularly its practical and experiential learning influence. This is an independent project:

- no formal affiliation, partnership, endorsement or representation of any Scout organisation is claimed;
- do not use Scout organisation names, logos, badges, ranks, uniforms or protected identity markers to imply affiliation;
- do not present products as official Scout materials or as approved by a Scout body;
- evaluate educational methods independently and retain only those consistent with learner autonomy, safety, inclusion and critical thinking.

The project does not inherit an organisation's authority, membership structure or institutional identity from its inspiration.

## Product application

For each product, record:
- master identity and publishing label;
- product name and edition/version;
- specific intended audience and use context;
- whether Fin and Nik or other recurring characters are used, and their instructional purpose;
- relevant educational influences, described as influences rather than affiliations;
- product-specific safety, privacy, accessibility and rights limitations.

This document governs brand usage; it is not a trademark clearance or legal opinion.
