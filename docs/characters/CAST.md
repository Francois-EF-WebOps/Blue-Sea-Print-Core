# Blue-Sea-Print-Core — Recurring Character Cast

**Status:** LOCKED — initial recurring cast  
**Decision:** Fin and Nik are the recurring character pair for Blue-Sea-Print-Core.  
**Scope:** This is the canonical identity and visual-continuity record. It does not certify 3D readiness, animation readiness, licensing, or commercial-release rights.

## Cast rule

- The initial recurring cast is **Fin + Nik**.
- Keep their identities, proportions, signature clothing, accessories, and established personalities consistent across products, covers, thumbnails, illustrations, previews, and content.
- Do not replace, merge, rename, or casually redesign either character.
- Additional characters may be introduced only when a real product or audience need arises and the cast decision is deliberately revised. They are not part of the initial cast by default.

## Fin — island boy

**Canonical identity:** 4-year-old island boy.

**Visual anchors**
- Deep brown skin.
- Coily dark hair.
- Freckles.
- White linen tunic.
- Wooden fishing rod as a signature prop.

**Continuity rule:** Preserve the childlike proportions, face, hair texture, skin tone, linen clothing, and fishing-rod identity. Do not infer new costume or cultural details without an approved reference.

**Source status:** Existing Fin reference set and two GLB candidates have been located in owner-controlled storage. The two GLBs have not yet been structurally compared; neither is designated the production master.

## Nik — island girl

**Canonical identity:** 8-year-old island child; caring big-sister figure from an island village.

**Visual anchors**
- Warm brown skin and visible freckles.
- Long, dark, loosely tied hair.
- Large brown eyes and a gentle, observant expression.
- Beige woven tunic/dress with blue-green embroidered neckline, sleeve edges, and hem.
- Woven basket carried over the shoulder, containing gathered fruit and flowers.
- Bare feet.

**Character traits**
- Kind, reliable, observant, thoughtful, and quietly brave.
- Helps gather fruit, herbs, and flowers and looks after younger children.
- Protective when family or friends are in need.

**Approved reference set**
- Character profile / model sheet: supplied by the owner in the project conversation.
- Full-body island-environment illustration: supplied by the owner in the project conversation.

**Continuity rule:** The character sheet is the identity and construction reference; the environmental illustration is a presentation reference. Preserve Nik's face, freckles, hair silhouette, woven garment, blue-green trim, basket, and barefoot design. Do not let environmental lighting or painterly rendering alter the underlying character design.

## Shared art direction

- Island/coastal setting with a warm, natural, lived-in atmosphere.
- Handcrafted materials and botanical details should feel coherent with the characters' established world.
- Keep Fin and Nik recognisable at small sizes: retain their signature silhouette, face/hair, costume, and prop cues.
- Use the approved character references as the source of truth. New poses, expressions, crops, and scenes must remain recognisably the same characters.
- Avoid unapproved style drift, costume swaps, age changes, skin-tone changes, or accessory substitutions.
